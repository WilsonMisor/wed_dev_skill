# Plans and Entitlements

Separate commercial plan labels from enforceable product entitlements.

## Define

1. Plan identifiers and lifecycle.
2. Entitlement identifiers.
3. Limits, quotas, features, seats, storage, or usage allowances.
4. Trial behaviour.
5. Upgrade and downgrade timing.
6. Grandfathered plans.
7. Administrative grants or overrides.
8. Expiry and revocation.
9. Server side enforcement points.
10. Audit and support visibility.

Do not gate valuable or sensitive capabilities only in the UI. Server and background processes must enforce applicable entitlements too.
