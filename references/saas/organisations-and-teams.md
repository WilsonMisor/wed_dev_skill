# Organisations and Teams

Use when accounts contain organisations, workspaces, teams, departments, or similar collaboration boundaries.

## Define

1. Organisation owner model.
2. Membership lifecycle.
3. Team hierarchy if any.
4. Role scope at organisation and team levels.
5. Resource ownership.
6. Transfer of ownership.
7. Leaving and removal behaviour.
8. Organisation deletion.
9. Limits and entitlements.
10. Audit events for membership and privilege changes.

Prevent removal of the last required owner unless a safe transfer or administrative recovery path exists.
