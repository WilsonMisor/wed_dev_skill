# SaaS Authentication

Define identity lifecycle across all clients and APIs.

## Cover

1. Signup or invitation based access.
2. Email or identity verification.
3. Password, passwordless, social, enterprise SSO, or federation methods.
4. Multi factor authentication when required.
5. Session and refresh token lifecycle.
6. Password or credential reset.
7. Account recovery.
8. Device or session revocation where required.
9. Suspended, disabled, deleted, and compromised accounts.
10. Audit events and anomaly detection.

Authentication establishes identity only. Every protected operation still requires authorization in the target tenant and resource context.
