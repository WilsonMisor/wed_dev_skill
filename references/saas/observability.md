# SaaS Observability

Extend core observability with tenant and service health.

## Track

1. Request success, latency, and error rate.
2. Authentication and authorization failures.
3. Queue depth, retries, and dead letters.
4. Background job duration and failure.
5. Billing and webhook processing health.
6. Database saturation and slow queries.
7. Cache behaviour.
8. Storage and integration failures.
9. Tenant impacting incidents.
10. Business critical workflow completion.

Tenant context in telemetry must support diagnosis without leaking one tenant's data to another tenant or unauthorised operators.
