# SaaS Billing

Billing is a financial state machine and requires explicit product and accounting rules.

## Define

1. Billable customer or tenant.
2. Currency and tax handling requirements.
3. Billing interval.
4. Invoice lifecycle.
5. Trial and promotional treatment.
6. Proration.
7. Credits and refunds.
8. Failed payments and dunning.
9. Grace period and suspension.
10. Cancellation and final invoice.
11. Reconciliation between provider and internal records.
12. Audit evidence.

Do not invent tax, accounting, or regulatory rules. Route uncertain financial interpretation to an accountable human specialist.
