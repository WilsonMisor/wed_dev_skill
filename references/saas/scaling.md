# SaaS Scaling

Scale from measured constraints and plausible growth, not speculative complexity.

## Evaluate

1. User and tenant growth assumptions.
2. Request volume and traffic shape.
3. Database size, query patterns, and connection limits.
4. Background job volume.
5. Storage growth.
6. Search and analytics workload.
7. External provider limits.
8. Hot tenants and noisy neighbour risk.
9. Cache requirements.
10. Horizontal and vertical scaling options.
11. Cost implications.
12. Operational complexity.

Define thresholds that trigger the next scaling intervention rather than prematurely adopting distributed components.
