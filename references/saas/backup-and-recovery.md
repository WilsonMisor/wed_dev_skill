# SaaS Backup and Recovery

Backups are useful only when restore is possible and tested.

## Define

1. Systems and data covered.
2. Recovery Point Objective.
3. Recovery Time Objective.
4. Backup frequency and retention.
5. Encryption and access control.
6. Geographic or provider separation where required.
7. Database, file, configuration, and critical metadata coverage.
8. Restore procedure.
9. Restore testing schedule.
10. Verification of restored integrity and tenant boundaries.
11. Responsibility and escalation.

Document data that cannot be restored and its operational consequence.
