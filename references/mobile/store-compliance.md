# Mobile Store Compliance

Store rules change. Verify current platform requirements before submission.

## Maintain

1. Accurate application description and screenshots.
2. Privacy policy and data declarations aligned with actual behaviour.
3. Permission explanations aligned with actual use.
4. Account deletion or management requirements where applicable.
5. Payment and digital goods rules applicable to the product.
6. Content rating and restricted content declarations.
7. Third party SDK data practices.
8. Reviewer access instructions.
9. Export or encryption declarations where required.
10. Contact and support information.

Do not rely on this blueprint as a substitute for current Apple or Google policy verification at release time.
