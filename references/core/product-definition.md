# Product Definition

Turn discovery into a bounded product definition.

## Define

1. Target users and jobs to be done.
2. Core value proposition.
3. Primary workflows.
4. Business rules.
5. MVP boundary and later scope.
6. Success metrics and guardrail metrics.
7. Operational responsibilities.
8. Support model.
9. Known legal, privacy, accessibility, security, financial, or contractual constraints.
10. Product assumptions and evidence gaps.

## Scope discipline

Every requested feature must be classified as required now, required later, optional, rejected, or unresolved. Do not allow implementation to treat brainstorming as committed scope.

Material product changes after approval require change control and downstream impact analysis.
