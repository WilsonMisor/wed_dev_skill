# Privacy and Data Handling

Apply when personal, confidential, regulated, or sensitive data is processed.

## Required decisions

1. Data categories and purpose.
2. Lawful or contractual basis as determined by an accountable human specialist where required.
3. Collection minimisation.
4. Access roles and privileged access.
5. Storage locations and subprocessors.
6. Retention and deletion.
7. Export, correction, account deletion, and consent behaviour where applicable.
8. Logging and analytics data treatment.
9. Encryption in transit and at rest where required.
10. Incident and breach response responsibilities.
11. Test and development data rules.
12. Data residency and cross border constraints where applicable.

Do not invent legal conclusions. Surface unresolved interpretation for human review.
