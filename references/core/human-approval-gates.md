# Human Approval Gates

AI is not the accountable approver for consequential product delivery decisions.

## Mandatory human decisions

1. Product baseline and major scope changes.
2. Legal, tax, regulatory, contractual, privacy, and accessibility interpretation when material.
3. Security risk acceptance for serious findings or sensitive controls.
4. Production access and final deployment.
5. Destructive or irreversible data operations.
6. Financial flows, payments, billing, escrow, or external spending.
7. Launch scope design approval where an owner or client approval gate is required.
8. Real user communications with material consequences.
9. Vendor contracts and paid commitments.
10. Final launch readiness.

Every approval must identify the artifact, version, evidence, approver, outcome, date, and any exception conditions.
