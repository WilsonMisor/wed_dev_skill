# Incident Response

Production incidents require controlled diagnosis, containment, recovery, and learning.

## Lifecycle

1. Detect and classify severity.
2. Assign incident owner.
3. Preserve evidence and establish timeline.
4. Contain user, security, financial, or data impact.
5. Diagnose root cause before speculative repeated fixes.
6. Recover service safely.
7. Verify recovery and monitor recurrence.
8. Communicate through approved channels.
9. Complete post incident review.
10. Convert findings into requirements, tests, monitoring, documentation, or architecture changes.

Security and privacy incidents must follow applicable escalation and notification requirements determined by accountable humans.
