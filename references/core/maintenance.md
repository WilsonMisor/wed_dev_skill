# Maintenance and Operations

A released product remains governed.

## Ongoing work

1. Dependency and platform updates.
2. Vulnerability management.
3. Backup and restore verification.
4. Monitoring and alert review.
5. Capacity and cost review.
6. Data retention and deletion jobs.
7. Certificate, domain, secret, and credential rotation.
8. Integration health.
9. Accessibility and browser or device compatibility drift.
10. Performance regression.
11. Support issue analysis.
12. Documentation freshness.
13. Product analytics and outcome review.

Maintenance changes use the same classification, task packet, review, test, and release discipline proportional to risk.
