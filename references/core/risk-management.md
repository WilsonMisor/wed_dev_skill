# Risk Management

Maintain a live risk register for material product, engineering, security, privacy, compliance, delivery, vendor, financial, and operational risks.

## Each risk records

1. ID and description.
2. Cause and consequence.
3. Likelihood and impact using the project scale.
4. Affected requirements or components.
5. Mitigation.
6. Contingency.
7. Owner.
8. Target date.
9. Current status.
10. Evidence and residual risk.

High consequence uncertainty must not disappear into meeting notes. Link it to an owner and a decision or validation action.
