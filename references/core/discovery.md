# Discovery

Discovery establishes the real problem before solution design.

## Required investigation

1. Problem statement and evidence.
2. Users, buyers, operators, administrators, support staff, and affected third parties.
3. Current workflow and pain points.
4. Desired outcomes and measurable success.
5. Constraints, dependencies, policies, budgets, timelines, skills, hosting, devices, and integrations.
6. Existing systems and data sources.
7. Risks, assumptions, unknowns, and decisions needing validation.
8. What is explicitly out of scope.

## Evidence rule

Separate facts, user statements, assumptions, estimates, and hypotheses. Do not convert a hypothesis into a requirement without recording its status.

## Output

Create a Discovery Record that can be traced into the PRD. Unresolved assumptions with high impact must receive an owner and validation action.
