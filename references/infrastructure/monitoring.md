# Monitoring

Monitor user impacting health and critical dependencies.

## Define

1. Availability checks.
2. Latency and error metrics.
3. Database health.
4. Queue and worker health.
5. Storage health.
6. Integration health.
7. Certificate and domain expiry where operationally important.
8. Resource saturation.
9. Backup job health.
10. Business critical workflow indicators.
11. Alert thresholds and routing.
12. Runbooks and owners.

Avoid alerts that fire frequently without an actionable response. Tune from real incidents and operating experience.
