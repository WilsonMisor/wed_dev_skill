# Hosting

Choose hosting from availability, latency, compliance, operations, cost, and deployment requirements.

## Document

1. Provider and services.
2. Regions.
3. Runtime and version constraints.
4. Database and storage services.
5. Network exposure.
6. Scaling model.
7. Backup and recovery features.
8. Logs and metrics.
9. Support and operational ownership.
10. Cost drivers.
11. Vendor specific dependencies.
12. Exit or migration considerations for critical workloads.

Do not assume a managed service removes your responsibility for application configuration, access control, backups, and restore testing.
