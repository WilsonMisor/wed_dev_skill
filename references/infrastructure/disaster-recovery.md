# Infrastructure Disaster Recovery

Define recovery from provider, region, credential, network, data, or deployment disasters.

## Required elements

1. Critical services and dependency order.
2. Recovery Point and Recovery Time Objectives.
3. Alternate capacity or rebuild strategy.
4. DNS and certificate recovery.
5. Database and storage restore.
6. Secret and credential recovery.
7. External integration reconfiguration.
8. Communication and decision owners.
9. Verification of data and tenant boundaries.
10. Scheduled exercise or tabletop testing proportional to risk.

A recovery plan that has never been exercised should be treated as unverified.
