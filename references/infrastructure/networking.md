# Networking

Define network boundaries according to trust and exposure.

## Cover

1. Public endpoints.
2. Private services.
3. Database exposure.
4. Administrative access.
5. TLS termination.
6. DNS.
7. Firewall or security group rules.
8. Load balancers and reverse proxies.
9. Egress requirements.
10. Webhook ingress.
11. Rate limiting and WAF where applicable.
12. Remote administration and VPN or private access where warranted.

Databases and internal management services should not be publicly reachable without a justified and secured requirement.
