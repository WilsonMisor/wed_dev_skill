# Database Selection

Choose storage technology from data and operational requirements.

## Evaluate

1. Data relationships and integrity needs.
2. Transaction requirements.
3. Query patterns.
4. Expected scale and growth.
5. Consistency requirements.
6. Availability and recovery needs.
7. Team operational skills.
8. Hosting constraints.
9. Security and compliance.
10. Cost and portability.

Prefer a well understood relational database for relational transactional business data unless requirements justify another model. Avoid adding multiple databases without distinct responsibilities.
