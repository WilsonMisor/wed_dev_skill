# Data Governance

## Govern

1. Data ownership.
2. Data classification.
3. Canonical definitions.
4. Access control.
5. Data quality.
6. Lineage where required.
7. Retention and deletion.
8. Export and portability.
9. Test data use.
10. Analytics and secondary use.
11. External sharing and processors.
12. Auditability.

For important metrics and business fields, define one authoritative meaning so web, mobile, reports, billing, and analytics do not silently calculate different values.
