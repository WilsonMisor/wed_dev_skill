# Data Retention

Retention must be defined for active records, deleted accounts, logs, audit records, backups, files, analytics, and external processors as applicable.

## For each category define

1. Purpose.
2. Retention period or trigger.
3. Legal or contractual basis if applicable and human approved.
4. Deletion or anonymisation method.
5. Backup expiry behaviour.
6. Exceptions such as legal hold.
7. Owner.
8. Verification.

Do not retain sensitive data indefinitely merely because storage is inexpensive.
