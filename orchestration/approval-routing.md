# Approval Routing

Every hard gate needs an accountable owner. AI can prepare evidence and recommendations but cannot be the sole approver for consequential decisions.

## Human approval required

1. Product scope baseline and major scope change.
2. Legal, tax, regulatory, privacy, accessibility, or contractual interpretation.
3. Security risk acceptance for critical or high findings, sensitive data, authentication, authorization, encryption, tenant isolation, or production access.
4. Production data migration with destructive or irreversible effects.
5. Payment, billing, escrow, money movement, and financial control decisions.
6. Design baseline for launch scope user facing experience when a product owner or client approval gate exists.
7. Vendor purchase, external spending, or paid service commitment.
8. Real user communications when consequences are material.
9. Production deployment and final launch.

## Gate record

Record gate ID, artifact or decision, owner, evidence, outcome, date, exception if any, mitigation, expiry for temporary risk acceptance, and next allowed state.
